Ezer:
The One Who Helps

Ezer is the Hebrew word for “one who helps.” LaEzer’s philosophy begins with this name: helping each person discover their own unique beauty. We aim to be a company that customers and business partners alike can rely on.

Founded in Daejeon, Korea in 2024, we entered Mongolia as our first export market and are now preparing to expand into Russia, Central Asia, Japan and Vietnam.

Systematic R&D grounded in scientific evidenceWe design precise formulas from skin science and ingredient efficacy data, and objectively verify efficacy and irritation through human application and irritation tests
Precise ingredient blends tailored to each skin concernWe analyze the properties and role of each ingredient and develop formulas whose complementary blends deliver what the skin needs
Harmony between nature and modern scienceBy combining naturally derived ingredients with modern skin science, we offer skincare that nurtures the skin’s healthy balance and helps restore its natural radiance

How to Use

Morning & Evening Care Routine

Use the ampoules and the cream together

Morning Routine

Light, and fast to absorb
1
Cleanse & Tone

Wash your face and prep the skin with toner.

2
Callus Repair Ampoule

Fills with moisture. Absorbs quickly and leaves a soft glow.

3
Cream (thin layer)

Spread a thin layer to lock in moisture.

4
Sunscreen

Apply once the cream has absorbed.

In the morning, spread a thin layer and apply sunscreen once it has absorbed.

Evening Routine

Intensive overnight care
1
Cleanse & Tone

Wash away the day and prep the skin.

2
CICA Spicule Ampoule (every other day)

Refines skin texture. It may tingle, so avoid the eye and lip area.

3
Callus Repair Ampoule (generous)

Apply generously to focus on hydration and firmness.

4
Cream (generous)

Seals in moisture overnight and protects the skin barrier.

Apply generously before bed and the moisture lasts until morning.
