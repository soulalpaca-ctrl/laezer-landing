Why LaEzer

The real reason
your skin wavers first when seasons change

Daily temperature swings of more than 10°C between seasons, and indoor air dried out by summer cooling and winter heating — everyday environments like these put strain on the skin barrier, day after day.

As moisture escapes, skin dries from within and grows more sensitive, leading to fine lines, enlarged pores and a rough texture. This is a skin-barrier problem that an ordinary surface moisturizer alone cannot solve.

Sudden temperature changesDaily swings of more than 10°C between seasons put repeated strain on the skin barrier
Dry indoor airIndoors, where cooling and heating lower the humidity, skin loses its inner moisture
A weakened skin barrierWith less ability to block external irritants, skin becomes sensitive easily
